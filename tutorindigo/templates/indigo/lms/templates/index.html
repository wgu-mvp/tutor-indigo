Not Sure Your Skill Level?


Which one are you?



Am I a Beginner?


Understanding: Knows prompts are instructions for AI; sees how input shapes output.


Skills: Writes simple, clear prompts (e.g., "List 5 fruits").


Competency: Avoids vague wording; matches basic intent to results.


Focus: Clarity and basic prompt structure.

Am I Intermediate?


Understanding: Sees how phrasing and context affect AI responses.


Skills: Crafts multi-step prompts (e.g., "Summarize this, then explain it simply").


Competency: Adjusts prompts to fix poor outputs; handles creative tasks.


Focus: Consistency and guiding AI with purpose.

Am I Advanced?


Understanding: Grasps AI limits and strengths; knows advanced techniques.


Skills: Designs complex prompts (e.g., "Act as an expert, analyze X, create Y").


Competency: Controls outputs precisely; solves multi-step problems.


Focus: Precision, efficiency, and real-world applications.
